# Email Templates

以下模板仅用于 `dry-run` 或人工确认后的首封发送。

## 中文首封模板

主题：

`帮帮｜AI 驱动的 C2C 本地上门服务平台｜Pre-Seed 融资`

正文：

```text
您好，

我联系您，是因为贵机构在 [投资方向/公开 thesis] 上与 BangBang 高度匹配。

BangBang（帮帮）是一款 AI 驱动的 C2C 本地上门服务平台，口号是“有事找帮帮”。我们希望让普通用户既可以下单，也可以用自己的时间、技能和地理位置获得收入。

我们当前在准备 Pre-Seed 融资，计划融资人民币 200 万元，拟出让 20% 股权。资金将主要用于产品研发、市场冷启动、团队搭建、基础设施和合规。

如果贵机构对 AI 应用、本地生活、Marketplace 或 Future of Work 方向感兴趣，我们很希望安排一次 20-30 分钟交流，并在会前提供更多材料。

谢谢。
```

## English First Email Template

Subject:

`BangBang | AI-native C2C local services marketplace | Pre-Seed`

Body:

```text
Hi,

I am reaching out because your public investment focus appears relevant to BangBang.

BangBang is an AI-native C2C local services marketplace. Our goal is to let ordinary users both request help and monetize their time, practical skills, and proximity.

We are currently preparing a Pre-Seed round and are seeking RMB 2 million for 20% equity. The funds will primarily support product development, go-to-market, team building, infrastructure, and compliance.

If your team invests in AI application, marketplace, local services, or future-of-work opportunities, we would appreciate the chance to schedule a brief 20-30 minute conversation and share more materials.

Best regards,
BangBang
```

## 个性化要求

- 第一段必须说明为何联系该机构
- 不得虚构 traction、营收、用户量
- 不制造虚假紧迫感
- 不在首封里展开条款谈判
